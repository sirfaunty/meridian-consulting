---
title: "Three signals it's time to rethink your operating model"
date: 2026-01-20
description: "Growth hides a lot of problems — until it doesn't. Three signs your operating model has quietly stopped serving the business."
---

Every operating model has a shelf life. The structure that got you to $5M in revenue
is rarely the one that gets you to $20M. Here are three signals the model has quietly
expired.

## 1. Every decision routes through one or two people

When the founders or a couple of leaders are still the bottleneck for decisions,
growth stalls not for lack of demand but for lack of throughput. The fix is rarely
"work harder" — it's clearer ownership and decision rights.

## 2. Your best people are doing work three levels below them

Senior talent absorbing junior work is a tax you pay in both salary and morale.
It usually means roles and processes haven't kept pace with headcount.

## 3. Nobody can name the top three priorities

If you asked ten people for the company's top three priorities and got ten different
answers, you don't have an execution problem — you have a clarity problem. That's
the cheapest thing to fix and the most expensive to ignore.

If two or more of these sound familiar, it's worth a conversation.
