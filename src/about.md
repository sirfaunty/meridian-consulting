## Who we are

Meridian is a boutique advisory firm founded in 2016 by operators who spent their
careers inside high-growth companies — not just studying them. We bring that
hands-on perspective to every engagement.

## How we work

We keep teams small and senior. The person you meet is the person who does the
work. Engagements are scoped tightly, run in weeks not quarters, and always end
with something your team can own and run without us.
